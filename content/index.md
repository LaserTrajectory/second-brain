# Motivations

As an incoming graduate student, and a PhD hopeful, it's incredibly important that I maintain some semblance of organisation throughout the rest of my intellectual journey. I'm sure that there have been so many ideas, thoughts, or articulations that have slipped through my mind in the last few years, and the lack of any sort of pressure to seriously organise beyond the bare minimum has resulted in me having little to no real structure to any of my work.

That's changing now, and this is the result of that – a real second brain, that acts as a storeroom for all of my explorations, and hopefully the foundation of my eventual [[thesis]] work.

# Who am I?

I'm Aniruddh. As of writing this note (December 2025), I've been accepted to 1 Master's program in data science and public policy (I won't say which yet – I haven't gotten all my acceptances yet, and haven't decided where I'm going to go).

BUT this means one thing – I can finally start preparing for my long-term goal of actually working at the intersection of data science and public policy. This means building, learning, studying, exploring – and I don't have a lot of time to do it.

As I write this, of course, things will evolve, but we can hope that this can serve as a foundation for my own understanding of my thought process as it evolves.
