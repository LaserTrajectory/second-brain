# State Capacity as a Determinant of Policy Outcomes

As Professor Lant Pritchett, and Professor Karthik Muralidharan (both of whom are on my [[prospective mentors]] list) argue, state capacity/capability is central to actually building the human capital necessary to sustain institutionally driven growth.

Studying state capacity, and how [[Dynamic Policy Feedback Loops]] could support improvements in fidelity of such governance systems, is central to the theoretical foundation of my [[thesis]].
