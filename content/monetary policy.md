**Sunday, December 14, 2025**

This note contains my thoughts and curiosities surrounding monetary policy, which is a field that I find interesting, despite my relative lack of familiarity with it.

It serves as a great structural analogue for my core [[thesis]] idea of [[Dynamic Policy Feedback Loops]], which talks about how policymakers should have a continuous stream of information regarding society, and make continuous tweaks to localised regulation, rather than making sweeping changes to a policy, hoping to cover everything but allowing things to slip through the cracks. This is similar in effect to how monetary policy acts as an incremental balancing mechanic, in comparison to fiscal policy which is a sweeping set of changes.
